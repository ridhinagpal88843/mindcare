Small things that make a difference

Simple self-care ideas for your everyday student life.

🚶

Take a little walk

Step outside for 10 minutes. A change of environment can give you a refreshing break.

💧

Drink some water

Keep a water bottle nearby and take regular sips throughout your study sessions.

📵

Take a screen break

Put your phone away for a few minutes. Give your eyes and mind a little rest.

🎵

Listen to music

Play something calming or uplifting that helps you feel comfortable.

🌙

Protect your sleep

Try to maintain a regular sleep routine. Rest is an important part of wellbeing.

💬

Talk to someone

Reach out to a friend, family member, mentor, or someone you trust.

If you are struggling, consider speaking with a qualified mental health professional, your college counsellor, or someone you trust. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In India, you can call 112 for emergencies or Tele-MANAS at 14416 for mental health support.
